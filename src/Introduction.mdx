# @lablup/ui-common

Lablup's UI layer on top of [Astryx](https://github.com/facebook/astryx): the
Astryx surface re-exported 1:1, the Lablup brand theme, and the components
Lablup products share.

The stories here cover ui-common's own components. Astryx's components are
documented by Astryx.

## Toolbar

- **Theme**: the Lablup brand theme, or Astryx's neutral baseline. A component
  that only looks right under Lablup is assuming the brand.
- **Locale**: the locale handed to Astryx's `InternationalizationProvider`,
  with Astryx's and ui-common's catalogs. Built-in strings follow it.
- **Dark mode**: the moon icon. It switches the `mode` of `<Theme>`.
